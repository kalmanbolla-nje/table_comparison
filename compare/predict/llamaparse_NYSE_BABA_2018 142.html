The expense arising from share-based awards relating to Ant Financial granted to our employees represents a non-cash charge that will not result in any economic costs or equity dilution to our shareholders. We believe that the grant of these equity awards to our employees will encourage mutually beneficial cooperation between us and Ant Financial. We expect that our share-based compensation expense will continue to be affected by the change in fair value of our shares, our subsidiaries' share-based awards and the quantity of awards we grant to our employees and consultants in the future. Furthermore, our share-based compensation expense will also be affected by the anticipated increase in fair value of share-based awards of Ant Financial. As a result of these factors, we expect that our share-based compensation expense will likely increase.
