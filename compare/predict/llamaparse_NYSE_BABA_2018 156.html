Our sales and marketing expenses increased by 44% from RMB11,307 million in fiscal year 2016 to RMB16,314 million in fiscal year 2017. The increase was primarily due to the consolidation of Youku and Lazada, as well as an increase in advertising and promotional spending mainly to promote our business initiatives, such as Tmall Supermarket and UCWeb during fiscal year 2017 and an increase of RMB1,222 million in payroll and benefit expenses.
